📚 Semester 1 Units

The Teacher’s Book identifies three units before the Learning Club section.

🎬 Unit 1 — Entertainment

Lesson 1 — Jobs in entertainment

Vocabulary: presenter, performer, comedian, clown, puppeteer, sound editor, stunt performer, animator, writer, costume designer, make-up artist, camera operator.

Lesson 2 — WOW! Talent Show

Read and listen about Emily, Bayan, Frank and Majid and their roles in the talent show. Practise describing entertainment jobs.

Lesson 3 — Grammar and communication

Practise Present continuous for arrangements and Present Perfect with for and since.

Lesson 4 — Skills practice

Develop reading, listening, speaking and writing around entertainment.

Lesson 5 — Project / English in Action

Use the entertainment topic in practical communication. The digital resources include the Unit 1 English in Action video about buying tickets.

Lesson 6 — Culture

Culture lessons develop Omani identity and awareness of the wider world.

🎯 Unit 1 Quick Check

Which person tells jokes and makes people laugh?

“I’ve worked here ___ five years.”

🦁 Unit 2 — Awesome Animals

Lesson 1 — Animal body parts

Vocabulary: antennae, beak, flippers, paws, horns, scales, spines, whiskers.

Lesson 2 — Amazing animals

Read and listen about animals and use clues such as body parts, movement and appearance to identify them.

Lesson 3 — Grammar: possibility and certainty

Practise could, may, might, must and can’t to make deductions about animals.

Lesson 4 — Skills and animal facts

Reading, listening, speaking and writing include animal fact files and descriptions. The unit practice uses the axolotl as an example.

Lesson 5 — Project / English in Action

Use animal information to communicate and present ideas. The digital resources include a Unit 2 English in Action video.

Lesson 6 — Culture

Culture develops awareness of Oman and the wider world.

🔎 Animal Detective

It has fur and only one hump. It must be an ...

It has scales. You are not sure. It ___ be a fish.

✈️ Unit 3 — Travel

Lesson 1 — Travel and transport

Expressions: travel by train, catch a bus, get off, pack my suitcase, take a seat.

Lesson 2 — Holiday equipment

Vocabulary: passport, guidebook, medicine, umbrella, suitcase, raincoat, charger, flip-flops, pillow, washbag, toothbrush, soap.

Lesson 3 — Grammar: future plans and predictions

Practise will and be going to. Unit practice also revises present continuous, present perfect, first conditional and modals.

Lesson 4 — Skills practice

Reading includes travel emails and information texts. Writing includes a holiday plan and an email reply.

Lesson 5 — Project / English in Action

Use travel language in practical situations and communicate plans.

Lesson 6 — Culture

Culture develops awareness of Oman and the wider world.

🧳 Travel Quick Check

Which sentence is correct?

What do you do before getting on a train?

✏️ Grammar Practice

Interactive revision based on Semester 1 grammar in the Teacher’s Book.

Unit 1 — for or since?

I’ve worked at the aquarium ___ five years.

We’ve lived here ___ 2017.

Unit 2 — Animal deductions

It has a beak and wings. It ___ be a bird.

I’m not sure what it is. It ___ be a fish.

Unit 3 — will or going to?

Look at those clouds! It ___ rain.

I think we ___ have a great trip.
